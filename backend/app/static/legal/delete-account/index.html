GuardianPD — account deletion requests

Any guardian, school staff member, or School Admin can request that their GuardianPD account and associated personal data be deleted. This applies whether you use the mobile app, the School Admin web console, or the Master Admin console.

To request deletion:

Email us from the address associated with your account, including your name, your role (Guardian / School Staff / School Admin), and your school's name. We'll confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Email info@de-ronimak.com to request deletion

What gets deleted

  • Guardians: your name, email, phone, QR credential, and any children's records linked only to you are permanently deleted, along with your attendance history at that school.
  • School Staff: your staff login is permanently deleted.
  • School Admin / whole-school requests: a school can request deletion of its entire account, which removes every guardian, child, staff, and attendance record at that school.

What we may keep

Where required for legitimate recordkeeping (for example, resolving an active billing dispute, or a legal obligation), we may retain minimal records for a limited period after your deletion request, as described in our Privacy Policy. We do not retain deleted personal data for any purpose beyond that.

A note for guardians

Because a child's attendance record involves both a guardian and the school responsible for that child, a request to delete only your own guardian account may also require confirmation from the school if you're the only guardian linked to a child — we'll let you know if that applies when you contact us.

The service

GuardianPD lets a school ("Customer") enroll guardians and children, issue signed QR credentials, and record drop-off/pick-up events by scanning those credentials. Guardians receive notifications when their child is scanned in or out. Full details of what data this involves are in our Privacy Policy.

Accounts and responsibilities

  • A school's Master Admin account is created when the school enrolls. That School Admin is responsible for the accuracy of guardian and child records entered into the system, and for managing School Staff accounts.
  • School Staff accounts may only be used to scan QR credentials for attendance purposes.
  • Guardians are responsible for keeping their printed or shared QR credential secure and reporting a lost credential to the school promptly.
  • You agree not to use GuardianPD to enroll or track individuals without the appropriate authority to do so (e.g., a school enrolling a child in its own care, a parent/legal guardian activating their own account).

Billing

Schools are billed 500 NGN per enrolled child per 30-day period, calculated automatically from the number of children enrolled at billing time. Payment is processed by Paystack. Invoices are payable by the due date shown; unpaid invoices may result in the school's subscription being suspended, which disables QR credential issuance and scanning until the account is brought current and reactivated. Suspension does not delete any data. See our Refund Policy for cancellations and refunds.
